About Bora Brews

Derived from the Korean word for purple ("Bora"), Bora Brews was created as a peaceful sanctuary for students, readers, and creatives.

We combine artisan espresso, quiet study desks, fresh botanical arrangements, and a community book corner under one cozy roof.
